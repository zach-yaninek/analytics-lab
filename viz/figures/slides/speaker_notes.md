## htm_01_concentration_curve.png

**The top 5% of devices hold 33% of tickets; chance alone gives 22%**

Full chart: `viz/figures/htm_01_concentration_curve.png`

- Population: 6,621 devices in the exposure population (shared internal host and devices with no host excluded), 5,395 unplanned tickets Aug 2025 to Jul 2026.
- Chance = 1,000 Poisson draws at each device's expected count (its machine type's rate × months active). The band is the middle 95% of the draws; for the top 5% the 95th percentile is 22.8%.
- Gini over devices: 0.725 observed vs 0.602 by chance. Devices with 6 or more tickets: 106 observed vs 15 by chance.

## htm_10_ticket_rate_by_machine_type.png

**sbsx-51 has the highest ticket rate of the 19 types shown: 2.9× the fleet**

Full chart: `viz/figures/htm_10_ticket_rate_by_machine_type.png`

- Machine-month = one month of a machine in service. Each telemetry device counts the months of Aug 2025 to Jul 2026 that fall within its first-to-last-transaction span, both end months included and quiet months in between too, so a machine active throughout adds 12. Devices with no transaction in that window count 0 months but stay in the device counts and rates. Devices on the shared internal host or with no host are excluded.
- Shown: the 19 types with at least 100 machine-months (the analysis's minimum for a type to get its own rate). Not shown: 7 smaller types (136 machine-months, 141 tickets), too unstable to compare.
- Fleet rate 7.5 = 5,395 tickets over 71,559 machine-months, all types; about 0.9 tickets per machine per year.
- Blue = the three types with the most tickets in raw counts (chart 9). They run only modestly above the fleet rate (scrubex2.0-2 +12%, scrubex2.0-3 +9%, scrubex2.0-4 +0%): their lead in raw counts is mostly fleet size.
- 95% Poisson intervals show how precise each rate is; they understate uncertainty because machines of one type differ from each other.
